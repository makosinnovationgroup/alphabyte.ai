## SEO Requirements (per post)

Each post will have:
- **Title** under 60 chars (before " — Alphabyte" suffix)
- **Excerpt** 140-160 chars with primary keyword naturally included
- **Primary keyword** in H1, first paragraph, and at least once more in body
- **2-3 internal links** to relevant service/tool pages with descriptive anchor text
- **relatedService** sidebar pointing to the most relevant Alphabyte service
- **BlogPosting JSON-LD** and **BreadcrumbList** (auto-generated by page wrapper)
- **Canonical URL** with trailing slash (auto-generated)
- **No forbidden vocabulary** (unlock, leverage, transform, etc.)
- Author: `adam-nameh`
