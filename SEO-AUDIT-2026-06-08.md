### What Shipped Since May 7 (verified)

- /services/citizen-development/ vs /services/executive-enablement/ Day-30 language differentiated (refutes May 7 finding)

### Top 5 Critical / High-Impact Gaps

1. **Comparison blog posts are still prose-only.** No HTML table in any of the three "vs" posts. Every ranking competitor for "Claude vs ChatGPT Enterprise" uses a structured feature matrix. Single highest-leverage content fix.
2. **Schema entity graph is fragmented.** Service.provider, Service.about.provider, and Person.worksFor all use inline Organization objects instead of `{"@id": "https://alphabyte.ai/#organization"}`. Person nodes lack their own `@id`. The org graph does not connect.
3. **Organization sameAs still only LinkedIn.** No Crunchbase, Microsoft Partner directory, Anthropic Academy, or Google Business Profile cross-references. AI engines and Google use sameAs to triangulate entity confidence.
4. **modifiedDate is in schema but invisible to readers.** Every post has a `modifiedDate` field that emits as `dateModified` in BlogPosting JSON-LD but never renders on-page. Quality raters cannot see freshness.
5. **Service pages contain zero third-party stats.** Citizen Dev, Executive Enablement, Discovery, and Data Readiness pages cite no McKinsey/Deloitte/Gartner data. AI engines have nothing to extract as an authoritative passage.

### Top 5 Quick Wins (under 1 hour each)

1. Add legalName + alternateName + expanded sameAs to Organization schema (`src/app/layout.tsx`)
2. Surface "Updated [date]" on blog post headers when modifiedDate differs from publishedDate (`src/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` — change to `dateAndReadTime` string)
3. Switch all inline Organization references to `@id` pointers across Service / Tool / Person / Article schemas
4. Add a 3-column HTML comparison table to claude-vs-chatgpt-enterprise.mdx (the highest-traffic comparison post)
5. Add `analytics.ahrefs.com` to CSP `script-src` and `connect-src` (defensive — currently only works because of `'unsafe-inline'`)
